Mysteries & Anomalies

Project Blue Beam

A modern conspiracy theory claiming that advanced projection, psychological operations, sky phenomena, and staged revelation could be used to reshape public belief.

Evidence: Level 06 - Modern Lore Status: Alleged plan / conspiracy theory Reliability: Unverified, heavily disputed Shelf: Mysteries & Anomalies

Core Claim

Project Blue Beam is usually described as a hidden plan to stage a mass spiritual, extraterrestrial, or apocalyptic event through advanced technology. In common versions of the theory, artificial sky displays, holograms, psychological messaging, religious imagery, and false disclosure could be used to guide humanity into a new political or religious order.

The theory is most often connected to Serge Monast, a Canadian writer who popularized the Blue Beam narrative in the 1990s. There is no public documentation proving that such a program exists, so the topic belongs in the archive as modern lore rather than documented history.

Common Claimed Stages

Claimed Stage

Destabilizing Existing Belief

Some versions claim the first step would involve discoveries or revelations that challenge established religions, ancient history, or public trust in inherited records.

Claimed Stage

Sky Projections

The best-known Blue Beam claim involves giant projected images or staged celestial signs appearing in the sky, interpreted as religious figures, alien craft, or supernatural events.

Claimed Stage

Directed Messaging

Another claim says voices, thoughts, or messages could be transmitted psychologically or technologically, creating the feeling of divine, alien, or inner revelation.

Claimed Stage

Managed Crisis

Many versions end with the idea that a staged threat or revelation would push the public toward centralized authority, a new world religion, or a controlled disclosure narrative.

Careful Reading

A careful archive should separate three things: real technologies that can influence perception, real government interest in psychological operations, and the specific Blue Beam claim that a unified staged global revelation exists. The first two have documented parallels in history; the third remains unproven.
