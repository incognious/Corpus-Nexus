Welcome to Corpus Nexus: a living archive for lost records, disputed histories, hidden traditions, strange sciences, forbidden texts, and the questions that survive when certainty gets too comfortable.

This page is a quiet doorway into the project. It is not listed in the main navigation, so it can be shared as a personal invitation before someone enters the full archive.

The purpose is not to force belief. The purpose is to gather fragments, compare claims, preserve sources, and keep the deeper questions open without losing the trail of evidence.
