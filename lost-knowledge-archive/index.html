Corpus Nexus gathers forbidden texts, old-world architecture, hidden traditions, lost technologies, disputed histories, and modern anomaly research into one connected archive. Each entry separates documented facts from interpretation and speculation, so the mystery stays alive without losing the trail of evidence.
