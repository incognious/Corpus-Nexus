Christian Mysticism

Theosis: Becoming United with God

A teaching page on the ancient Christian idea that salvation is not only forgiveness, but transformation into communion with God through Jesus Christ.

Status: Theology study notes Reliability: Ancient Christian teaching Section: Teachings

Main Teaching

Theosis means becoming united with God by grace. It does not mean a human being becomes the Creator, replaces God, or becomes equal to God. It means the soul is healed, purified, illumined, and drawn into communion with God while remaining fully human.

In this teaching, Jesus Christ is central. The Son of God joined divine life and human life in the Incarnation, opening the way for humanity to be restored, healed, and brought back into living union with God.

Union

Union with God

Union with God is the heart of theosis. It is not merely knowing facts about God, but being joined to Him in love, prayer, obedience, worship, and grace. The person remains human, but the heart becomes more alive to God's presence.

Christian language often describes this as communion: a shared life with God rather than distance from Him.

Healing

Healing of the Soul

Theosis sees sin as more than rule-breaking. Sin wounds the soul, darkens perception, disorders desire, and separates the person from God. Healing of the soul means the heart is slowly restored to truth, humility, peace, and love.

This healing includes repentance, forgiveness, prayer, discipline, mercy, and the grace of God working deeper than human effort alone.

Christ-Likeness

Becoming More Christ-Like

To become more Christ-like is to be shaped by the life of Jesus Christ: humility, mercy, truth, courage, forgiveness, purity of heart, and surrender to the Father. Theosis is not abstract spirituality; it becomes visible in character.

The sign of real transformation is not spiritual pride, but love, patience, repentance, and compassion.

Grace

Being Filled with Divine Grace

Grace is God's life and help given freely. In theosis, a person is not transformed by willpower alone. Divine grace heals, strengthens, illumines, and draws the person toward God.

Prayer, scripture, worship, fasting, confession, forgiveness, and acts of mercy are ways the heart opens itself to grace.

Image

Restoring the Image of God in Humanity

Genesis says humanity was made in the image of God. Theosis teaches that this image is not destroyed, but it can be buried, wounded, and obscured. Restoration means the image becomes clearer again.

As the soul is healed, the person begins to reflect God's goodness more truly: wisdom, love, creativity, mercy, holiness, and freedom from slavery to destructive passions.

Communion

Moving from Sin and Separation into Communion

Theosis is the movement from isolation into communion. Sin separates the person from God, from others, and even from the deepest truth of the self. Communion restores relationship.

This movement is not instant perfection. It is a path of returning: repentance, prayer, forgiveness, surrender, and steady union with God through Jesus Christ.

Simple Summary

Theosis is the ancient Christian teaching that human beings are meant to be transformed by grace: healed in the soul, restored in the image of God, made more like Jesus Christ, and brought from separation into communion with God.

Practice Note

A simple theosis prayer can begin like this: Lord Jesus Christ, heal what is wounded in me. Restore Your image in me. Fill me with Your grace. Bring me out of separation and into communion with You.
