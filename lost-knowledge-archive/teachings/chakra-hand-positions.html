Hand Positions for Chakra Meditation

A practical reference for using simple hand positions, breath, and attention while meditating through the seven chakra centers.

Status: Practice notes Reliability: Tradition varies Section: Teachings

Before You Begin

Chakra meditation comes from Indian spiritual traditions, while many modern hand-position systems blend yoga, tantra, mudra practice, and Western energy work. Use this page as a gentle practice map, not as a claim that every tradition teaches the same exact sequence.

Sit comfortably. Breathe slowly. Keep the hands relaxed. If a position causes pain, release it. The hand position is a focus tool; the real work is attention, breath, humility, and steadiness.

Seven-Center Practice

Center Focus Hand Position
Root Grounding, safety, body, stability Thumb and index finger touching on each hand, palms resting on knees.
Sacral Emotion, creativity, flow, desire Hands in lap, palms upward, right hand resting in left, thumbs lightly touching.
Solar Plexus Will, courage, discipline, personal fire Fingers joined in front of the stomach, thumbs crossed or touching, elbows relaxed.
Heart Compassion, forgiveness, love, balance Prayer hands at the chest, thumbs touching the sternum.
Throat Truth, voice, restraint, clear speech Fingers interlaced loosely, thumbs touching and lifted slightly.
Third Eye Discernment, perception, inner seeing Hands near the lower chest, middle fingers touching and pointing upward, thumbs touching.
Crown Surrender, prayer, divine attention Hands open on the lap or raised gently, palms upward in receptivity.

Simple Sequence

Step 01

Ground

Feel the body and breathe into the lower belly. Let the spine rise without becoming stiff.

Step 02

Move Slowly

Spend one to three minutes with each center. Do not rush to feel something dramatic.

Step 03

Close Cleanly

Return both hands to the heart or lap. Breathe normally. Notice the room, the body, and the present moment.

Caution

If meditation brings fear, dissociation, panic, or overwhelming sensation, stop and ground yourself. Walk, drink water, speak with someone steady, or return later with a simpler practice.
