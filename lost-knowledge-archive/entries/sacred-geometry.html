Hidden Traditions

Sacred Geometry

The study of circles, ratios, patterns, and forms treated as symbolic maps of creation, harmony, order, and hidden structure.

Evidence: Level 04 - Symbolic Status: Symbolic tradition Reliability: Documented forms, meanings vary Shelf: Hidden Traditions

Visual Forms

Core Forms

Circle

Unity and Eternity

The circle represents wholeness, heaven, cycles, protection, and the unbroken. Many geometric systems begin with a point and a circle.

Vesica

Creation Through Overlap

Two circles meeting create the Vesica Piscis, a symbolic doorway between two worlds, two forces, or heaven and earth.

Flower

Pattern and Multiplication

The Flower of Life uses repeated circles to suggest growth, harmony, and the unfolding of order from a simple form.

Triangle / Square

Spirit and Matter

Triangles often point toward ascent, fire, or trinity. Squares often represent earth, foundation, matter, and stable order.

Connections

Sacred Geometry links strongly to Symbols and Their Meanings, the Emerald Tablet, the Great Pyramids, the Akashic Records, the Black Sun / Saturn page, and Theosis. It sits between mathematics, architecture, mysticism, and symbolic theology.
