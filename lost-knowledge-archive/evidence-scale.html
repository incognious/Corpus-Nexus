Evidence Scale

A guide to the evidence labels used across Corpus Nexus, separating documented facts from disputed interpretation, symbolism, and speculation.

Status: Site method Reliability: Editorial framework Section: Reference

Why This Matters

Corpus Nexus covers topics that mix history, religion, folklore, science, symbolism, testimony, and speculation. The goal is not to flatten everything into one certainty level. The goal is to show what kind of claim is being made.

Level 01

Documented

Supported by primary sources, official records, museum records, manuscripts, photographs, reliable reporting, or direct historical evidence.

Level 02

Supported Interpretation

A reasonable reading based on evidence, context, and expert discussion, but still open to debate.

Level 03

Disputed

Real evidence may exist, but the meaning, timeline, cause, or interpretation is contested.

Level 04

Symbolic

Best treated as meaning, metaphor, ritual language, or spiritual interpretation rather than a direct physical claim.

Level 05

Speculative

An interesting possibility or theory that lacks enough evidence to treat as established.

Level 06

Modern Lore

A story, interpretation, or framework that spreads through online research communities, disclosure culture, or alternative-history circles.

Research Rule

The strongest pages show all three layers: what is documented, what is debated, and what is speculative. Mystery does not require pretending all claims have the same weight.
