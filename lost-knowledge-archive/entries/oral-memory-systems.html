Tradition

Oral Memory Systems

Knowledge preserved through story, song, place, ritual sequence, and trained recall rather than written archives.

Evidence: Level 01 - Documented Status: Documented in many cultures Reliability: Varies by tradition Shelf: Hidden Traditions

Why It Matters

Some knowledge systems were never lost from the inside. They only became invisible to outsiders who expected libraries and ledgers.
